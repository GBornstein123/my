# Open Loops: 90-Day Audience Doubling Plan

**Show:** Open Loops with Greg Bornstein: Conversations That Bend
**Prepared:** July 9, 2026

## Where the show stands today

- **292 episodes**, a deep back catalog most shows never reach
- **4.83★ across 59 Apple reviews**, strong listener connection, small review count
- **25 keywords ranking in Apple's top 10** (per PodSEO), but the show **stopped ranking for "supernatural abilities," "intellectual topics,"** and other discovery terms in the last 30 days
- Ranking well in Apple Podcasts **Nigeria** (per Podstatus), international audience exists
- Hosted on **Simplecast**, website on **Podpage**, social automation via **Missinglettr**
- **Missinglettr has been failing on every new episode since at least May 25** ("site doesn't match" error), automated social promotion has been dead for ~6 weeks
- Warm inbound guest pitches sitting unanswered: Stacey Chillemi (5x Dr. Oz guest), Dr. Daryll, and Behold Retreats (you said yes in April but it was never scheduled)

## The math of doubling

Doubling doesn't come from one tactic. It compounds:
~25% from fixing broken discovery + promotion, ~35% from guest-audience swaps,
~25% from being a guest on other shows, ~15% from back-catalog + review flywheel.
Each is realistic on its own; together they double.

---

## Phase 1: Stop the bleeding (Week 1)

1. **Fix Missinglettr.** Every episode since late May has published with zero social
   promotion. In Missinglettr settings, update the connected site URL to match the
   Simplecast blog URL (`open-loops-with-greg-bornstein-conversations-that-bend.simplecast.com`)
   or point it at the RSS feed directly. This alone restores a full promotion channel.
2. **Recover slipping keywords.** Log into PodSEO, pull the list of lost terms, and
   work "supernatural abilities"-type phrases back into the show description and the
   next 4 episode titles/descriptions. Episode titles are the #1 lever for in-app search.
3. **Send the 3 guest replies** (drafts already in Gmail): Stacey Chillemi, Dr. Daryll,
   Behold Retreats. Every booked guest = access to their audience.

## Phase 2: The guest flywheel (Weeks 2-6)

4. **Make audience-sharing a booking condition.** Every guest commits to sharing the
   episode with their list/socials. Make it easy: send each guest a ready-made kit
   (3 pull-quote graphics, 1 clip, suggested copy) the day the episode drops.
5. **Book 2 "audience-carrier" guests/month**, people with real email lists or
   followings in hypnosis, consciousness, paranormal, psychology. The inbound pitches
   show booking agencies already see the show as legit; flip the dynamic and go outbound
   to bigger names (podcast guests booked via PodMatch/MatchMaker are the low-effort path).
6. **Be a guest yourself, 2x/month.** "Greg The Magic Man," hypnotist with 292 episodes
   of material, is a strong pitch for hypnosis/paranormal/mindset shows. Appearing on
   shows your size or bigger is the single highest-converting growth tactic in podcasting,
   because listeners of podcasts are already podcast listeners.

## Phase 3: Compounding assets (Weeks 4-12)

7. **Review push to 100+.** Add a 15-second mid-roll ask ("If the show has ever bent
   your reality, leave a review. It's how new listeners find us"). Rotate the ask;
   59 → 100+ reviews improves both social proof and ranking.
8. **Clips pipeline.** Every episode → 2–3 vertical clips (YouTube Shorts, TikTok,
   Reels). The niche (hypnosis demos, "other side of reality" moments) is unusually
   clip-friendly. Tools: Opus Clip or Castmagic against the Simplecast files.
9. **Back-catalog resurfacing.** 292 episodes = a content mine. Re-promote one
   evergreen episode per week as a "From the Vault" social post; group the catalog
   into themed playlists/starter packs on the Podpage site ("Start here: Hypnosis,"
   "Start here: The Unexplained").
10. **Feed swaps & promo trades.** Pitch 4–6 similar-sized shows in the space for
    trailer swaps (you run their 60-sec promo, they run yours). Free, and directly
    reaches proven podcast listeners.
11. **Lean into Nigeria/international.** The show ranks well there. Mention it on air,
    engage those listeners, ask them for reviews on their storefront; momentum in
    one country's chart feeds the algorithm everywhere.
12. **Podcast Movement, Sept 17-18 NYC.** You're already on their list; it's local.
    Go. It's the densest room of cross-promo partners, bookers, and networks you'll
    be in all year.

## Measurement

- Pull the current 30-day download number from Simplecast analytics. That's the baseline.
- Track weekly: downloads (7-day per episode), Apple reviews, followers on Spotify,
  clip views, and guest-referred spikes.
- Target: +25–30% by day 45, 2x by day 90. If a channel isn't moving its number by
  week 4, cut it and reallocate to guesting, which has the best track record.
