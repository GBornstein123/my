# Minimum Qualification Criteria
## Tier 1 — Best Fit High Priority Customers

> Use this as the "must meet" checklist for FindIQ targeting.
> For each account, capture evidence (URL/snippet/notes) that supports each criterion.

---

## 1) Strategic Recognition of Knowledge & Enablement

**Description**
The organization acknowledges people-dependent operations and invests in knowledge transfer, upskilling, or digital enablement.

**Public signals (examples)**
- Digital transformation / AI initiatives
- CMMS, ERP modernization
- Mentions of tribal knowledge, SOPs, training, onboarding
- Roles such as: Head of Digital Transformation, Workforce Development, Engagement, Knowledge Management, Enablement
- Membership in associations (e.g., CESMII)
- Event/webinar participation

**Conversation signals (questions to validate)**
- "What initiatives exist today to capture and scale expert knowledge?"
- "Who owns training, enablement, or workforce development?"

**Evidence to capture**
- Initiative announcement, job posting, program page, press release, conference participation, internal enablement program mention.

---

## 2) Operational Complexity & Knowledge Transfer Risk

**Description**
The nature of operations makes knowledge transfer non-trivial and business-critical.

**Public signals (examples)**
- Multiple plants, shifts, regions
- Many machine types / variants
- Broad maintenance or reliability hiring
- Language such as: "manufacturing footprint", "production network"
- Process industry characteristics (issues not only mechanical; also process/material related ones)
- Downtime issues & measurement

**Conversation signals (questions to validate)**
- "Where does operational knowledge live today—and where does it break down across sites or teams?"

**Evidence to capture**
- Footprint statements, multi-site operations, reliability org growth, downtime/OEE language, complex asset mix.

---

## 3) Buying Access & Process

**Description**
There is a credible path into the buying center and budget ownership is discussable.

**Public signals (examples)**
- Target persona bridges ops & leadership (e.g., VP Ops, Plant Manager)
- Cross-functional or staff roles exist (digital transformation mgmt, AI, operational excellence, workforce development/knowledge transfer)

**Conversation signals (questions to validate)**
- "How are decisions like this typically funded and approved?"
- "What budget discretion do you personally have?"
- "Who else would need to be involved?"
- "What criteria do you base your decisions on?"

**Evidence to capture**
- Known champions, org chart signals, buying group roles present, procurement/process maturity indicators.

---

## 4) Timing & Trigger (3–6 months)

**Description**
A concrete near-term event turns strategic relevance into operational urgency.

**Public signals (examples)**
- New production lines or facilities
- Expansions, reshoring announcements
- AI pilots (e.g., Copilot), standardization programs
- Event/webinar participation
- Retiring experts
- Onboarding of new people
- Critical machine-caused downtime / bad quality

**Conversation signals (questions to validate)**
- "What changes in the next 3–6 months would make this a priority now?"
- "Is there an initiative this would naturally plug into?"

**Evidence to capture**
- Press releases, capex announcements, modernization programs, hiring spikes, transformation timelines.

---

## 5) Firmographics & Scale

**Description**
Company size justifies complexity, budget, and a structured buying process.

**Public signals (examples)**
- Revenue estimates (LinkedIn, Crunchbase, annual reports)
- Employee count
- Number of plants / installed base

**Tier guidance (as written in the criteria)**
- Tier 1 Manufacturer or OEM: $1B revenue, 5 production sites/plants, 5K+ employees
- Tier 2 (upper mid-market manufacturers, mid-market OEMs): $250M+ revenue ($100M OEM); 2–4 plants (n.a. for OEM); 1–5K employees (250+ OEM)

**Conversation signals (questions to validate)**
- "How many sites or customer operations does your team support today?"
- "How big is your service/maintenance team?"

**Evidence to capture**
- Public filings, credible estimates, multi-site footprint, maintenance org size.

---

## 6) Industry Fit

**Description**
Company operates in an industry where operational complexity and knowledge loss materially impact performance. Excludes industries with extreme regulation or long sales cycles.

**Focus industries (examples)**
- Industrial
- Food & Beverage
- Plastics
- Metal
- Packaging
- MedTech
- Semiconductor
- Mobile machinery

**Avoid (as written in the criteria)**
- Regulated industries, declining industries

**Conversation signals (usually not needed; if unclear)**
- "Which part of the value chain creates the most operational risk today?"

**Evidence to capture**
- NAICS/industry classification, product lines, manufacturing process profile.

---

## 7) Geographic Focus & Accessibility

**Description**
Company operates in target regions with strong manufacturing density and openness to digital / AI adoption.

**Public signals (examples)**
- HQ or major plants in DACH (DE, AT, CH)
- EU proximity markets (Nordics, Benelux, N. Italy)
- US presence in Midwest, Southeast, Texas

**Conversation signals (questions to validate)**
- "Where are your most critical plants or service operations located today?"

**Evidence to capture**
- Facility locations, regional org footprints, hiring locations, plant announcements.
