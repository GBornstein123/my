# Open Loops: On-Brand Guest Targets

The brief: obscure conspiracy authorities, Geocities-era UFO webmasters, and people
who built (or rode) time machines in the government. Checked July 2026 for who is
alive, active, and bookable.

## Bucket 1: Government time machines

1. **Andrew Basiago** (Project Pegasus). Attorney who claims DARPA/CIA teleported him
   as a child, including to Gettysburg in 1863. The archetype of this genre, still
   actively doing podcasts (The Why Files covered him in 2025). Book via projectpegasus.net.
   Clip potential is off the charts.
2. **Ron Mallett** (UConn professor emeritus). The legitimate counterweight: a real
   theoretical physicist who has spent his life trying to actually build a time machine
   with circulating lasers, driven by grief for his father. Active on podcasts as of
   late 2025. Reach via UConn physics emeritus contact. Pair with Basiago as a
   two-episode arc: "the man who says he did" and "the man trying to."
3. **Stewart Swerdlow** (Montauk Project claimant). With Preston Nichols, Al Bielek,
   and Peter Moon's circle mostly gone, he is the last major living Montauk voice.
   Verify current booking contact via expansions.com before pitching.
4. Bench: Randy Cramer and Tony Rodrigues (Secret Space Program "20 and back"
   claimants), David Lewis Anderson (Anderson Institute time-warp field research).

## Bucket 2: Geocities-era UFO web at its height

1. **John Greenewald Jr.** (The Black Vault). Started FOIA-ing the government at age 15
   in 1996 and built the largest civilian archive of declassified UFO documents.
   Very active, big audience, easy contact form at theblackvault.com. Highest
   audience-swap value on this list.
2. **Peter Davenport** (NUFORC director since 1994). The man who answers the national
   UFO hotline. Confirmed active in 2025 (AARO's UAP workshop, Contact in the Desert).
   Endless stories of 30 years of 3 a.m. calls. Book via nuforc.org.
3. **Royce Myers III** (ufowatchdog.com and its "Hall of Shame"). The era's internal
   affairs cop who policed UFO frauds from inside the community. Obscure and perfect.
   Activity unverified, needs a dig.
4. **Jeff Rense** (rense.com, sightings-era radio). Flag: his site carries genuinely
   toxic content beyond UFO material. Skip unless you want that fight.
5. **Episode idea instead of a guest:** "Elegy for the UFO Web." Glenn Campbell of
   ufomind.com, the best UFO site of the 90s, died in 2021, and hobbyists have since
   restored his 400,000-link archive. Interview an archivist restoring the dead UFO
   web. Perfectly on brand and nobody else has done it.

## Bucket 3: Obscure conspiracy authorities

1. **Joseph Matheny** (Ong's Hat). Creator of the proto alternate reality game about
   an interdimensional travel cult in the Pine Barrens. Thirty years later people
   still believe it. The "did you open a portal or a story" conversation is literally
   an open loop. Reach via josephmatheny.com.
2. **Adam Gorightly** (self-described crackpot historian). Discordianism, Kerry
   Thornley and the JFK orbit, Manson-era high weirdness. Former podcast host, so
   he is mic-ready. Reach via gorightly.wordpress.com.
3. **Kenn Thomas** (Steamshovel Press). The parapolitics zine publisher, Maury Island,
   JFK and UFO crossover. "America's best loved conspiracy researcher."
4. **Greg Bishop** (The Excluded Middle zine, author of Project Beta, host of Radio
   Misterioso). Project Beta is the true story of the Air Force gaslighting Paul
   Bennewitz into a breakdown with fake UFO disinformation. Bonus: he has his own
   show, so this is also a cross-promo and feed-swap play. Reach via radiomisterioso.com.
5. **Robert Guffey** (Chameleo, OR Books). English lecturer at CSU Long Beach who
   documented a heroin addict allegedly gang-stalked by invisible DHS operatives
   with stolen cloaking tech. Credible-weird in exactly the Open Loops register.
6. Bench: Walter Bosley (ex-AFOSI, breakaway civilization and 19th-century airship
   mysteries).

## Why this list also fixes discovery

PodSEO says the show lost rankings for "supernatural abilities" and "intellectual
topics." These guests naturally put time travel, Montauk, Ong's Hat, government
cover-up, and supernatural abilities back into episode titles and descriptions,
which is what recovers those keywords.

Month 2: Matheny + Davenport.
Month 3: Bishop (cross-promo) + Mallett (the straight-physics bend).
Sprinkle Gorightly, Thomas, Guffey, Swerdlow through the fall.
